# handii.co - Product Overview
### A Simple Guide to Understanding What We Built

---

## What is handii.co?

**handii.co** is an online shop for a small handmade crafts studio based in India.

Think of it like this: imagine a talented artisan who makes beautiful things by hand — flowers from pipe cleaners, resin coasters, bracelets, alphabet keychains — and they need a proper online store where customers can browse, buy, and even place custom orders directly with the maker.

That is exactly what handii.co is.

---

## Who is it for?

| Person | How They Use It |
|---|---|
| Customer | Browses products, adds to cart, places orders, chats with the artisan for custom pieces |
| Admin (Studio Owner) | Manages the whole business — products, orders, custom requests, coupons, everything |
| Visitor | Can browse and shop without creating an account |

---

## What Can Customers Do?

### Shop for Handmade Products
Customers can browse a catalog of 75+ handmade products across 4 categories:

- Pipe Cleaner — Handmade flowers, bouquets, keychains, and charms
- Resin — Alphabet keychains, coasters, jhumka earrings, small charms
- Bracelets — Beaded bracelets, disc bead bracelets
- Pixel Art — Ready-made designs or custom pixel art

Each product shows:
- A photo (which changes when you click on a color option)
- Price and available colors
- Add to Cart button
- Stock status (In Stock / Out of Stock / Notify Me)

---

### Buy Combo Bundle Deals
The shop offers special combo bundles where 2 or more products are sold together at a discounted price. Each combo has an auto-scrolling photo slideshow.

---

### Cart and Checkout
- Add multiple products (with different colors/variants) to the cart
- Apply discount coupon codes
- Fill in name, phone, and delivery address
- Choose Cash on Delivery or Online Payment
- Place the order — it is saved and trackable

---

### Request a Custom (Bespoke) Order
This is a unique feature. Customers can request something completely custom-made:

1. Fill a form describing what they want (category, colors, specs, photos)
2. A DM chat thread opens between the customer and the artisan
3. The artisan reviews it and sends a price quote in the chat
4. Customer can accept or decline the quote
5. If accepted, the artisan crafts the piece

It works like WhatsApp for orders — direct, personal, and human.

---

### Customer Account
Customers can create an account to:
- Track all their past orders (with live status: Placed > Crafting > Shipped > Delivered)
- View their custom order requests and DM chats with the artisan
- Save multiple delivery addresses
- Have their cart saved between sessions

Login is simple: either email + password, or a one-time code for passwordless login.

---

### Subscribe for Drop Alerts
Customers can subscribe with their email to get notified when new products drop.
When they subscribe, they automatically receive a 10% welcome discount coupon (WELCOME10).

---

## What Can the Studio Owner (Admin) Do?

The admin has a full control panel with 8 sections:

### 1. Products
- Add new products (with photo, price, stock count, colors)
- Edit existing products (change price, restock, add color variants)
- Upload product photos from their computer or paste a URL
- Delete products that are no longer available

### 2. Combo Deals
- Create bundle deals by selecting which products to group together
- The system automatically calculates profit margins and warns if a combo is not profitable enough
- Admin can set a custom combo price and see savings vs regular price

### 3. Orders
- See all orders placed by customers
- Update the order status (e.g., move from Crafting to Shipped)
- See what was ordered, by whom, for how much, and where it is going

### 4. Custom Quotes
- See all bespoke requests submitted by customers
- Open the DM chat for any request
- Send a price quote with a breakdown and estimated days
- Decline requests with a reason

### 5. Coupon Codes
- Create discount codes (percentage off or flat amount off)
- Set rules: minimum order value, usage limit, per-customer limit, expiry date
- Turn coupons on or off at any time

### 6. Reels
- Add Instagram-style product showcase videos/photos
- Each reel shows the product handle (@handii.co) and view count

### 7. Reviews
- View customer reviews for products
- Moderate (delete) any inappropriate reviews

### 8. Newsletter Subscribers
- See who subscribed to drop alerts
- Copy all subscriber emails with one click (for bulk campaigns)

---

## How Does the Pricing Work?

Every product has two prices stored:

- Display price — what the customer sees (e.g., Rs.210)
- Cost price — what it actually costs to make (~45% of the selling price)

This lets the admin see real profit margins, especially when creating combo deals.
The system prevents creating a combo that is not at least 25% profitable.

---

## The DM Chat — How It Works

When a customer submits a custom order request, a private chat thread is created:

- The customer describes what they want (with reference photos they can upload)
- The admin can reply, ask questions, and send a formal price quote
- The quote shows as a special card in the chat with the price and estimated days
- The customer accepts to confirm — or the admin can decline with a reason
- Once accepted, the order moves into production

---

## How Are Orders Tracked?

Every standard order gets a unique number like HND-384729 and goes through:

  Placed > Crafting > Shipped > Delivered

Custom orders go through:

  Submitted > Quoted > Accepted > Crafting > Completed

Both the customer (in their dashboard) and the admin can see the status at any time.
